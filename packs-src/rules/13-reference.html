The Book of Stipulations is a guide for players and GMs alike in determining how things interact in the system: the esoteric rulings and odd interactions that are not properly documented and are found by playing a session and asking how something works.

How a ruling gets in

The Book holds various situations and their solutions, as agreed upon by the community's Mentor staff, and occasionally curated by Erin.

  • A Mentor resolves a situation or answers a question in the field, then records the interaction and how it plays out, as decided by at least 2 Mentors.
  • If a single Mentor answers a question, it cannot be put in the Book unless Erin or another Mentor agrees with how it would go. For the sake of brevity, when only one Mentor is available, that Mentor's ruling in the field is how it will go.
  • Players may pose their own questions, which can be answered in the same way.

Turn order and timing

At the start of its turn a unit qualifies for damage, healing, and a buff (Poison, Imbue, and an ally's White Pool, for example). What order do they resolve in? In the case of a buff that also damages the unit, like Sword Saint's Iaijutsu, is the damage and buff split, or simultaneous at the Buff stage of resolution?

Healing, then Damage, then Buffs, then Debuffs. So in the first instance, they would heal from Imbue, then get damaged by Poison, and then receive the buffs of White Pool.

Source note

The question as recorded notes it was first answered in play as “damage, healing, buffs”. The ruling above is the one entered in the Book.

Where in the combat do Aggressor and Resistor put their extra attack?

After normal attacks, such as the initiating attack and follow-ups.

If a unit initiates combat with a weapon that has 2x Strikes (such as a Brave weapon or Fists) and has the Aggressor skill, how many strikes does that additional attack deal?

1 Strike. Brave weapons do not need that much more of a buff.

Weapons

When a sidearm weapon (a weapon with the text “can be used to counterattack when holding a 2+ range weapon”) is used as intended to counterattack, when is it equipped?

It is equipped at the beginning of combat, and is used to determine things like Attack Speed for the purposes of Dodge. After combat and its effects resolve, the wielder returns to having the previous weapon equipped.

When a weapon breaks and turns into its Broken or Drained variant, is that automatically equipped, or are you forced to use the next weapon in your inventory, if applicable?

You continue using the equipped weapon (if proficient in that weapon type) until another weapon is equipped or the weapon is unequipped by another effect, such as Disarm or unequipping.

When a weapon is attacking with an atypical damage stat for its type, what defense stat is it targeting?

After discussion:

  • MAG, not STR: Resistance
  • STR, not MAG: Resistance
  • DEX, not STR: Defense
  • Attack Speed, not STR: Defense

Devil weapons are devilishly complex. How do they determine backfire damage?

The damage that would be taken on a backfire is the same damage you would have dealt with that attack. For example, if you were attacking an 8 defense target with your 24 damage Devil Sword attack, you would deal 16 damage to them, and on a backfire you take 16 damage instead, even if your defense were higher or lower than 8.

When a skill allows its user to use a weapon it normally could not, does this grant wEXP?

Unless specifically said, using a weapon does not grant wEXP. Examples include Animus/mose (which grant reduced wEXP, but still wEXP), Vulture (normal wEXP), and Rune (can cast spells, but does not give wEXP).

Is it possible to lose a Weapon Rank?

Outside of the reclassing system it is not really possible to lose a weapon rank. Even Practiced does not technically give you a rank (if that is the skill that gives you access to weapons one rank higher).

Skills

If using Longarm, does Thrower activate? Would a Slim Lance gain the +10 Hit and +5 Critical?

Longarm: “...attack with melee weapons at 2 Range ... attacks are not considered ranged...” Thrower: Unit gains +10 Hit and +5 Critical when using ranged melee weapons at 2+ range.

No.

In the case of skills with prerequisites (Imbue, Endure, Magekiller, Slayer) and Beorc's Racial Skill (5 to 10 Skill Capacity): if a unit fails to meet a prerequisite any longer, does the replaced skill have to follow Beorc's 5 to 10 Capacity rule? For example, Imbue (10 Capacity, meets Beorc's requirement) becoming Vital Strike (20 Capacity, does not).

Yes, it does.

Experience and growth

If you attack multiple enemies with a single attack, how is EXP calculated?

You gain EXP equal to the combat EXP of the highest amount you would earn from a combat between each of them, in addition to full kill EXP if any kills happen.

What is the absolute minimum a growth can be?

5%.
