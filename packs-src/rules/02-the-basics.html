All units have a variety of stats. The core of which are HP, STR, MAG, DEF, RES, DEX, SPD, LUK, MOV, CON, LoS, WT, INT and CHA.

Key rule

For all purposes, stats are always rounded down if applicable, and almost all negative numbers become 0 instead, barring one exception: Critical Avoid.

The core stats

HP
The unit's total Health Points. If a unit is reduced to 0 HP, they are incapacitated, or killed. See [[death]].
STR
Strength, determining their damage with physical weapons.
MAG
Magic, determining their damage with magical weapons.
DEF
Defense, which reduces damage taken from physical weapons.
RES
Resistance, which reduces damage taken from magical weapons.
DEX
Dexterity, which determines how accurate they are, their ability to score critical hits, and to activate most Skills. This may alternatively be known as Skill or SKL.
SPD
Speed, which determines their ability to dodge attacks and make follow-up attacks.
LUK
Luck, which slightly boosts a unit's accuracy and evasion, and determines their ability to perform and prevent critical hits.
MOV
Movement, which determines how far they can move in a turn. Units move in the cardinal directions, but not diagonally. Units can pass through their allies, but not enemies.
CON
Constitution, which determines the effect of their equipment's Weight on them, and their own Weight.
LoS
Line of Sight. This dictates how far the unit can see (or rather, what radius around the unit is revealed) in maps where vision is obscured or during fog.
WT
Weight. This represents how much the unit weighs, including their mount or the armor it is wearing. This determines what units can Shove or Rescue and who they can Shove or Rescue. WT is your CON plus your class's WT.
CHA
Charm. This is used for Ability Checks.
INT
Intelligence. This is used for Ability Checks.

Beyond the numbers

In addition to these, every unit has:

  • Skills, the powers and techniques it can use. See [[skills]].
  • An Affinity, which shapes its Supports. See [[affinity]].
  • Special Qualities from its class, such as being Armored or Mounted. See [[special-qualities]].
  • Proficiencies with weapons. See [[weapons]].
  • A Condition during maps. See [[conditions]].
  • For Laguz, a Shift Gauge. See [[non-beorc]].
  • And, if you count it as a stat, their Gold.

Range and Midrange

Range determines what spaces a unit can attack with a certain weapon or tome, with 1 Range being an adjacent tile, 2 Range being two tiles away, and so on. All Range values past 1 allow you to attack diagonally.

Certain weapons may also be Midrange. Midrange Weapons are able to attack adjacent tiles at “range”, such as a shorter ranged projectile weapon like a Crossbow, or a throwing weapon like a Francisca. In effect, these weapons can initiate at 1 Range without being countered, unless their opponent has a 1-2, 1-3 or similar Range weapon, or a Midrange weapon of their own.

If the weapon's actual Range is 1, it can counter melee and non-Midrange 1 Range attacks. If not, it cannot counter melee attacks.

Uses

Uses are the weapon's durability. Once a weapon's Uses reach zero, it breaks, turning into a Broken Weapon of the same type.

WeaponLoses a Use
Melee and thrown weaponsOnly when landing an attack.
Ranged weapons such as Bows and CrossbowsWhenever they make an attack.
Magical weapons, Tomes and StavesWhenever they make an attack. Staves do not lose Uses when they are used in melee, however.

Weapons can be repaired at places like Blacksmiths and Forges. Items, as in healing items and the like, also have Uses, but unless they are Shields, they simply disappear when their Uses reach zero.

Ruling · Book of Stipulations

When a weapon breaks and turns into its Broken or Drained variant, you continue using it (if proficient in that weapon type) until another weapon is equipped or it is unequipped by another effect.

Effects

Effects can grant stat boosts, Skills, or can inflict Conditions or deal heavy damage to Special Qualities.

Key rule

If a weapon has the name of a Condition in its Effect, it inflicts that Condition on hit.

If a weapon has the name of a Special Quality in its Effect, it deals Effective damage to units with that Special Quality, increasing the weapon's Might by 3×.

Proficiency

Proficiency is the unit's skill and familiarity with a weapon, tome or staff. Units may only equip and use weapons with the same Rank or lower as their Proficiency Rank with that weapon type.

There are 9 Ranks: F E D C B A S SS ★

F is equivalent to being nonproficient, and all your Proficiencies start at this Rank by default. S, SS and Star all represent increasing mastery over a given weapon type, and confer special bonuses to the unit whenever they use that weapon type.

Gaining Weapon Experience

Proficiency increases through usage of that weapon. Whenever a unit lands an attack with a weapon, the unit adds the wEXP value of the weapon to their Proficiency with that weapon type. If the attack is a killing blow, that attack gives double wEXP. Once a unit achieves enough wEXP, their Proficiency Rank with that weapon type will increase.

With your unit's stats and your weapon's stats, you arrive at your final Combat Stats: Damage, Accuracy, Critical, Attack Speed, Dodge, Critical Avoid, and Offensive Staff Hit and Avoid. These are the numbers you roll against.

Key rule

For all purposes, stats are always rounded down if applicable, and almost all negative numbers become 0 instead. The one exception is Critical Avoid.

Skills are various powers, techniques and the like that the unit possesses and can use.

Equipping Skills

Skills can be freely equipped or unequipped at any time out of combat, or at any other time the GM sees fit.

Key rule

Typically the maximum amount of Skills a unit can have at once is four, but at GM discretion this can be expanded.

Learning Skills

Skills can be found, as any item can be, upon Skill Scrolls, which can be used to teach the unit that Skill.

For Skills that have prerequisite Skills, such as Improved Vital Strike requiring Vital Strike, obtaining the upgraded version of that Skill will automatically replace the prerequisite, weaker version. This is so a unit cannot stack Weaponbreaker and Improved Weaponbreaker on one weapon type, or have access to two Vital Strikes per map.

Units also gain Skills from their class: a starting Skill at [[character-creation|creation]], and more when they [[promotion|promote]].

Skills that activate by chance

Many Skills have a chance to activate when you make an attack, and in other circumstances. Their chances vary greatly: some might be DEX%, or DEX/2%, or maybe LUK%.

Your chances increase by leveling that stat, or by obtaining a boost in Skill Activation Rate, which adds its value to all activation chances. As with hitting and critting, rolling equal to or below your activation chance on a D100 means you activate the Skill.

Special Qualities are something particular about the unit and their class. A Special Quality is something like being heavily armored, or a bandit, or having a mount.

Some Special Qualities do not inherently affect a unit's capabilities, while others do. For example:

  • Mounted and Flying units are able to use the remainder of their unused MOV after taking actions before ending their turn.
  • Armored units gain buffs when waiting and have a chance to improve the defenses of nearby allies.
  • Flying units can fly over most terrain and obstacles, ignoring both their penalties and positive effects, except for healing.
  • Ballisticians cannot be Rescued, but also move after acting.

Key rule

All Special Qualities are additionally weaknesses, rendering units with them vulnerable to certain weapons. A weapon that names a Special Quality in its Effect deals Effective damage to units that have it. See [[weapons]].

Canto+

Mounted, Flying and Ballistician. The unit can move the rest of their movement after:

  • attacking, using an item, trading or rescuing;
  • Refreshing, or using a staff;
  • using a skill action other than Hold or Bastion;
  • Shifting forms with Feral Instincts;
  • interacting with objects like doors or chests;
  • Visiting, Arriving (if the map does not end), and Seizing (if the map does not end).

Rampart

Armored. When the unit Waits, they gain buffs based on their Special Qualities, listed below.

When Waiting, allies 3 spaces from the unit gain a 35% chance to use this unit's DEF or RES (if higher) when taking damage, increasing to 55% at 2 spaces and 90% when adjacent.

Rouse

Beast. At the start of this unit's turn, the unit may gain buffs until the start of their next turn, based on their Special Qualities and listed below. Rouse has a 2 Turn cooldown on use.

Map Affinity

Alongside this, maps may have their own Affinity. If a unit's Affinity is the same as the map's, they receive a +5 bonus to Hit and Dodge.
