Once you have a character concept, building the unit takes eleven steps. Create a new unit, open its sheet, and work down the list.

Tip

It is a good idea to discuss what you are planning to play with your fellow players, and to balance your party, but be careful that nobody is forced into playing something they do not want to.

Step by step

  1. Pick your class

    Put down its base stats on your sheet. You may increase or decrease WT by up to 3.

  2. Choose where CHA and INT come from

    Choose two stats to serve as the basis for your CHA and INT: DEX, SPD or DEF for CHA, and MAG, DEX or RES for INT. You cannot use DEX for both. See [[ability-checks]].

  3. Take your starting Skills

    If the class has a starting Skill, put it in the first slot of your Skills. If it has multiple choices, pick one. All units, whether or not they have a starting Class Skill, also start with one Generic Skill.

  4. Set your Proficiencies

    Your class gives you Proficiency in one or more weapons: D Rank in one weapon of your choice and E Rank in any others. All Proficiencies start at 0 wEXP.

    Some classes with multiple Proficiencies can also give one up to make the other C Rank, or give up their D Rank (but not the proficiency) for an additional proficiency.

  5. Spend your Gold

    All units start with 1,500 Gold to buy equipment with. Buy as much as you want within that limit, and add it to your sheet.

  6. Assign 5 bonus points

    Bonus points permanently increase your stats. You cannot raise one stat by more than 2.

    • They can go into HP, STR, MAG, DEF, RES, DEX, SPD, LUK, CON, CHA and INT.
    • HP and LUK take 2 bonus points for each point they rise.
  7. Set your Growth Rates

    Growth Rates determine what stats increase when you level up, and you are free to tailor them to your needs.

    Growth Rates cap at 80% for HP and 60% for all other stats, but increases from items and the like can break these caps.

  8. Choose a Boon and a Bane If you want

    If you apply a Boon you must apply a Bane, and vice versa.

    Boon raises, Bane lowersStatGrowthStat cap

    You cannot Boon and Bane the same stat, cannot Boon or Bane CHA or INT, and cannot Bane a stat that is 0.

  9. Spend your ability points

    You gain ability points equal to 5 × the higher of CHA or INT to allocate to your abilities and saving throws, plus 5 extra points specifically for saving throws. An ability may only hold points up to 5 + your current level.

  10. Note your Affinity

    At character creation, characters are given an Affinity. It is like the unit's birthsign, and sets the stat boosts received by or provided to those you Support. See [[affinity]].

  11. Write the profile

    With the mechanics out of the way, write down your character's lore and personality, as well as your own notes, boundaries and thoughts as a player.

GM advice

Bonus points and Growth Rates can be very different from campaign to campaign, so it is advisable to tailor-make characters for a campaign and its rules, rather than adapting and remaking existing ones. The numbers on this page are the defaults. Players should still practise making a character or two with them.

In Foundry

Growth Rates live on the Growth tab of the unit sheet, ability points on the Abilities tab, and the profile on the Story tab.

What comes next

When leveling up, you roll a D100 for each stat, and if the roll is equal to or lower than your Growth Rate for that stat, it increases by 1. That is covered in [[levelling]], and what happens at Level 20 in [[promotion]]. Laguz, Manakete and Taguel have a few extra choices to make, described in [[non-beorc]].

Optional module

Several modules add choices at creation: [[opt-affinities|more Affinities]], [[opt-holy-blood]], [[opt-crests]] and [[opt-heart-seals|Heart Seal classes]].

The Non-Beorc classes follow similar rules to each other, but differ in key ways. All of them fight by transforming.

What they share

  • All of the Non-Beorc classes gain 50% to STR, MAG, DEF, RES, DEX and SPD upon transformation.
  • They all have unique weapon types, starting at D Rank and automatically changing to the next version upon gaining a Rank increase.
  • They do not promote like Beorc do. See [[promotion]] for how they advance instead.

Laguz and the Shift Gauge

A Laguz's Shift Gauge controls their ability to switch forms, from humanoid to animal. Each Laguz tribe has a different starting point and rate at which the gauge increases and decreases.

Key rule

Laguz can choose to transform once their Shift Gauge is at least 20/30, and may hold their Shift even at a full 30/30. They can revert to human form at any time when Shifted, but automatically do so when their Shift Gauge hits 0.

How the gauge moves

Starting Shift Gauge
What they have at the very beginning of the map. It may be changed at the GM's discretion.
Shift Modifier per turn
How much gauge they gain or lose passively at the beginning of their phase, depending on form.
Shift Modifier per combat
How much they gain or lose after a round of combat. Only the first combat per Phase gives the full amount, with all subsequent combats giving only 1.

Shifting is like Trading in that it does not take up your action, but you are unable to move after doing so. A Laguz can move to a target, Shift, then Attack.

By default, Laguz are unable to use Weapon Manuals, training weapons, or gain wEXP for standard weapons. This can be changed at the GM's discretion.

Source note

The Stats section says the gauge changes at the start of the unit's turn; the Non-Beorc section says at the beginning of their phase. Both wordings are kept above.

Laguz also have the Gregarious Special Quality, which lets two adjacent Laguz Shift together. See [[special-qualities]].

Manakete and Taguel

Manaketes and Taguel do not have a Shift Gauge. Instead, they are always in humanoid form, but transform whenever they are in combat.

  • They can only transform a limited number of times on any given turn, no matter the phase. They may transform up to 4 times per turn, but this number can be increased through use of items or Skills.
  • They may spend an additional transformation when initiating combat to gain the effect of Canto+ after combat. This transformation will be restored at the start of the next phase.
  • When starting their turn adjacent to a Non-Beorc class ally, Manaketes and Taguel gain an additional +10% increase to their stats when transforming until the start of their next turn.
  • They counterattack using Fists if unable to transform, but if able to through the use of Manuals or otherwise, will equip the next weapon they can use in their inventory.
