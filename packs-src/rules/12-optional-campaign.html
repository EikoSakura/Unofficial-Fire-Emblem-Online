If a Beorc has the Mounted or Flying Special Quality, they will be allowed to dismount and lose their Special Quality, instead walking on foot.

Optional module

[[opt-mounts]] builds on this module, and makes every class listed here need a Mount to ride.

Getting down

  • The unit loses its Mounted or Flying Special Quality. If they have the Dragon Special Quality, they will also lose this upon dismounting.
  • They take various stat boosts and penalties, unique to which class they are. See the table below.
  • All classes also lose 2 MOV when Dismounted, unless stated otherwise.
  • As they have no Special Quality, they will also take terrain bonuses and penalties as a unit with no Special Qualities would, including the movement penalties.

Getting back up

When remounting, they are not allowed to mount and take advantage of their increased movement before moving. They may only remount after moving and before taking an action.

Depending on the GM's decision, mounted units may be forced to Dismount when going indoors.

When Dismounting, classes may also have their Proficiencies changed. All Mounted units, when dismounting, lose access to their previous weapons and must instead use Swords. They also cannot use Swords while Mounted unless they gain the proficiency via Manual, Promotion or start out with it, such as a Sword Cavalier.

With this module, all classes subject to the Dismounting module require a Mount to ride.

Optional module

This module needs [[opt-dismounting]].

How Mounts work

  • Mounts have separately tracked HP, and take the same amount of damage as their rider does.
  • They cannot be healed except with Healing items outside of a battle map.
  • When a Mount's HP drops to zero, it dies and the rider is Dismounted until they acquire a new Mount.
  • During character creation, a character is assumed to have the Normal version of their class's Mount type, free of charge.
  • Any unit can, unless specified otherwise by the GM, only possess one Mount at a time, and must swap Mounts if they wish to use a new one.

Optional additions

Falling

If a flying class such as a Pegasus Knight or Wyvern Knight is forcibly Dismounted through Mount death over terrain they cannot cross on foot, such as a Peak or Ocean, the GM can rule that they are instantly killed from the impact, and this may extend to anyone they were carrying through Rescue or Capture, instead of harmlessly moving to traversable terrain.

This may also come into play even without Mounts or Dismounting if the unit is slain over a mountain and their passenger is incapable of traversing it.

Effective weapons

Effective weapons may have an innate chance to instantly kill a Mount. By default this is 25% per hit, but it may be raised or lowered by the GM.

This chance extends only to weapons that do not normally have that kind of Effectiveness, so only a weapon such as a Halberd would have it, and a Hunting Bow would only have an instant kill chance on Horses. Weapons like the Wing Clipper also only target Pegasi, even though they have effectiveness against Flying wyverns as well.

Units build up Fatigue as they fight. A unit becomes Fatigued when its Fatigue is greater than its maximum HP.

Optional module

GMs may change how Fatigue impacts units.

Being Fatigued

Key rule

When a unit is Fatigued, they suffer −3 to STR, MAG, DEF, RES, DEX, SPD, MOV and CON.

Gaining Fatigue

Fatigue is increased in multiple ways, and if a unit performs multiple of these at the same time, they will be added up.

Pushing past your limits

When gaining Fatigue, a unit may choose to attempt to push themselves past their physical limits. Make a DC 120 Endurance saving throw.

  • On a success, you take 1 less Fatigue.
  • On a failure, you take +1 Fatigue per Fatigue gained. For example, entering combat and using a Combat Art would incur an additional +2 Fatigue.

Losing Fatigue

Fatigue can be reduced by eating provisions, being healed by staves, or not being fielded for a map.

  • A unit regains half of their missing Fatigue + the tenths place of their Endurance saving throw if they are not fielded for a map.
  • Units revived by Valkyria or protected by an Aum Fragment will have all Fatigue restored.
  • Units revived by a Soul Ember will have half of their current fatigue restored.

With this module, rather than the standard Phase system, units will take their turns semi-simultaneously.

Optional module

This replaces the usual order of Player Phase, then the other factions' Phases.

Taking turns

When a Player unit moves, the other unit factions will move immediately after them, then Players will move. To put it simply: a player character moves, then an ally moves, then an enemy moves, then another player character moves, and so on and so forth until all units have moved, thus ending the full turn.

Uneven numbers

For situations where the amount of units is not 1 to 1, divide the amount of units in a faction by the amount of Player Units, rounded up. If there is a greater number of Player Units than Enemy Units (or whatever faction, like Neutral, is remaining), the equation is applied in reverse. This also applies to Ally units.
