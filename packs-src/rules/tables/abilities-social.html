DeceptionCHA + LUK/3 + Points
Used when a unit attempts to tell lies, deceive another, or other times the GM deems fit. Deception is usually an opposed ability check against Intuition.
DiplomacyCHA + LUK/3 + Points
Used when a unit attempts to convince others, resolve differences, gather information, or other times the GM deems fit.
Animal HandlingCHA + LUK/3 + Points
Used when a unit attempts to work with animals. This can be teaching tricks or commands, domestication, calming them, or other times the GM deems fit.
IntimidationSTR, MAG, or CHA + LUK/3 + Points
Used when a unit attempts to frighten others, forcibly have them act a certain way, or other times the GM deems fit.
IntuitionCHA + LUK/3 + Points
Used when a unit attempts to discern another’s true intentions, detect any falsehoods, predict someone’s next move, or other times the GM deems fit.
PerceptionINT + LUK/3 + LoS + Points
When a unit attempts to notice fine details, detect the presence of something, listen in on conversations, or other times the GM deems fit.
PerformanceDEX or CHA + LUK/3 + Points
When a unit attempts to provide entertainment, a distraction, any other form of performance, or other times the GM deems fit.
