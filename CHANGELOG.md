# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Six more Optional Modules, each a world setting that starts switched off: Stagger, Passive Staff Effects, Heal Criticals, Double-Cast, Staff Missing and the Follow-up Critical Multiplier.
- Stagger gives every unit a Stagger bar beside its HP, on the sheet, the battle HUD and the forecast. A unit whose bar is emptied is Staggered: it takes double damage and cannot act until the end of its next turn.
- The Follow-up Critical Multiplier is set for each unit on the Optional tab of its sheet, and shows in the forecast and on the combat card.
- Summoning: a unit with Summon puts its Phantom on a free tile next to it, with a weapon drawn for its level. The Phantom is a unit of its own that keeps to its summoner's level, and Multi-Phantom allows more than one on the map.
- Warping: Teleport and Agile Teleport let a unit pick a tile to warp to from the battle HUD, and the staves that move units (Warp, Rescue, Rewarp, Entrap, Push, Return, Miswarp) now move the token themselves.
- Trainees are created as the rules have them: no Generic Skill, every proficiency at E Rank and fewer growth points to place. On promoting, a Trainee takes its Generic Skill from the Skill Roll.
- A class can start one proficiency at a higher rank than D, as "1 at C rank" says, and the Creator follows it.
- Skills that give weapon ranks, such as Aspected and Practiced, raise the rank when taken and give it back when removed.
- Skills that come in several variants, or that raise growth rates of the unit's choosing, are chosen in the Creator.
- More skills are applied automatically in combat, among them Disarm, Ruin, Black Magic, Cudgel, Flense, Inferno, Fearsome, Flash Freeze, Dragon Ward, Anathema, Breath of Life and Prevail.
