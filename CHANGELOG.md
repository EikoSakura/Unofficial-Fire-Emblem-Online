# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- Skills on units made under an older version are brought up to date by themselves. When a Game Master opens the world after an update, every skill takes its Effect, its numbers, what the system does with it and its icon from the skills compendium, and keeps what is the unit's own: whether it is equipped, what was picked on it, and where its cooldown and uses stand. The Game Master is told in chat which skills changed, which now ask for a pick, and which weapon ranks to check by hand. Skills from a compendium of your own are left alone.
- Level Up splash: a level up is shown over the whole screen with the new level, the stats and what each gained, and a line from the unit. The lines for a great, a fair and a poor level are written on the Story tab of the sheet. Each player can switch the splash off for themselves.
- Chat cards are shorter: a card shows its headline and its results, and the magnifying glass on the card, or a right-click on the message, opens everything behind them, down to the dice as they fell.
- Optional Content: Katanas are in the game as a kind of Sword with a place of their own in the Weapon Triangle, along with the classes Ashigaru, Rei, Hitokiri, Oni Savage, Yokai, Shugoki, Merchant, Profiteer and Tycoon and their twelve skills, among them Income, Haggle, Hard to Kill and Wealth Of Power.
- Deployment zone: the Game Master marks the tiles the players set up on with the terrain brush. It is shown to everyone, and units stay inside it until the battle begins.
- Skill Capacity uses the raised values for all units: 30 Capacity Points, 60 from the second tier and 120 from the third.
- Heart Seal Classes are chosen from a menu on the sheet that lists every class a unit can start in, with a field to search them.
- A unit that waits for help in the Character Creator carries a raised hand beside its name in the Actors list.
- A new setting limits the Gold a skill such as Income gives in one map.

- The whole Skills list has been gone through, and nearly every skill is now applied by the system: in the forecast, in the fight, on the map and as turns pass. A skill that hangs on something the system cannot see says so on its sheet and is named in a fight to be applied by hand.
- New commands on the battle HUD: Shove and the skills that push, pull and swap units, Rescue and Drop, Refresh with its songs and dances, Steal, and Trade.
- Canto and its kin let a unit move again after acting, and skills such as Galeforce give it another go in the same turn.
- Shield Block: a Shield is equipped beside the weapon and blocks the hits it can, with its Block chance in the forecast and on the combat card.
- Skills that are chosen before a fight are switched on from the forecast, and skills that answer in the middle of one (dodge a hit, roll a miss again, soak a hit) ask their player with one click. A new setting lets the Game Master's units answer by themselves.
- Fights follow the skills that reshape them: attack order, extra attacks, further rounds, the Weapon Triangle, hits that splash onto the units around, and what a fight leaves on both sides afterwards.
- Skills used as an action are used from the battle HUD by picking a unit, a tile or an area on the map, and stances such as guarding, or stepping in for an ally, last until something ends them.
- Summons beyond the Phantom: undead minions, animals, Spirits, Mogalls, Terrors and reinforcements appear as units of their own, and go when their skill says so.
- Imitate takes on another unit's attributes for a few turns, and skills that lend a weapon put it in the unit's hands for the attacks it starts.
- Staves that reach several units, or reach further, now do, and more staff effects are applied by themselves.
- Doors, chests and Villages can be placed on a map by the Game Master and acted on by units; Mines and Traps are laid on tiles and spring on whoever steps on them.
- Weather: the Game Master sets the weather of a map from the Phase banner, and its movement costs, stat changes, damage, lightning and ballista fire are applied. Fog of War itself is left to the Game Master.
- Map Affinity: a map can have an Affinity of its own, and units of that Affinity gain Hit and Dodge on it.
- Additional Affinity Effects: all of them are applied, the ones a unit uses when it wants are switched on from its sheet or the battle HUD, and the rank or shorter cooldown some of them give is chosen on the sheet.
- Skill cut-ins: as a skill takes effect its icon and name flash over its unit, with a sound. Each player can set them to fast or off and mute the sound for themselves.
- A side with no units left in the fight no longer takes a Phase.
- Experience can be given to a unit from its sheet, multiplied by its skills and those of the units around it.

## 0.3.0

- Six more Optional Modules, each a world setting that starts switched off: Stagger, Passive Staff Effects, Heal Criticals, Double-Cast, Staff Missing and the Follow-up Critical Multiplier.
- Stagger gives every unit a Stagger bar beside its HP, on the sheet, the battle HUD and the forecast. A unit whose bar is emptied is Staggered: it takes double damage and cannot act until the end of its next turn.
- The Follow-up Critical Multiplier is set for each unit on the Optional tab of its sheet, and shows in the forecast and on the combat card.
- Summoning: a unit with Summon puts its Phantom on a free tile next to it, with a weapon drawn for its level. The Phantom is a unit of its own that keeps to its summoner's level, and Multi-Phantom allows more than one on the map.
- Warping: Teleport and Agile Teleport let a unit pick a tile to warp to from the battle HUD, and the staves that move units (Warp, Rescue, Rewarp, Entrap, Push, Return, Miswarp) now move the token themselves.
- Trainees are created as the rules have them: no Generic Skill, every proficiency at E Rank and fewer growth points to place. On promoting, a Trainee takes its Generic Skill from the Skill Roll.
- A class can start one proficiency at a higher rank than D, as "1 at C rank" says, and the Creator follows it.
- Skills that give weapon ranks, such as Aspected and Practiced, raise the rank when taken and give it back when removed.
- Skills that come in several variants, or that raise growth rates of the unit's choosing, are chosen in the Creator.
- More skills are applied automatically in combat, among them Disarm, Ruin, Black Magic, Cudgel, Flense, Inferno, Fearsome, Flash Freeze, Dragon Ward, Anathema, Breath of Life and Prevail.
